01 — Mandate

03 — Approach

How we build.

/ 01

Critical infrastructure should not depend on systems beyond national control. We design for European autonomy from the ground up.

In our domains, the barrier is not the prototype — it is the approval, the operational record, and the trust. We treat compliance as core engineering.

Every capability is built to serve both mission profiles. Defence rigour raises the civilian standard; civilian scale lowers the defence cost.
